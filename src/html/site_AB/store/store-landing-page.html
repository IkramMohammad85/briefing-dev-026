The Asia Briefing Publication Store is home to business magazines and guides for China, ASEAN, India, Vietnam and other key areas of emerging Asia. We also feature special issues which cover other countries and regions from time to time.

This is part of our integrated digital resource network for foreign investors in Asia. Our goal at Asia Briefing is to empower foreign investors by increasing the transparency of doing business in the region and to connect the business intelligence and developments between them.

In our publications, we harness the on-the-ground insights of Dezan Shira & Associates, a specialist foreign direct investment practice, providing corporate establishment, business advisory, tax advisory and compliance, accounting, payroll, due diligence and financial review services to multinational corporations investing in emerging Asia.
