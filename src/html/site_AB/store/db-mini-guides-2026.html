Market Entry and Setting Up

 Published: September 2026

Malaysia offers foreign investors a well-established business environment, strategic access to Southeast Asian markets, developed infrastructure, and a diverse industrial base. However, establishing a successful presence requires careful planning around market entry structure, ownership, licensing, taxation, employment, and ongoing compliance.

Human Resources and Payroll

 Published: September 2026

Businesses employing staff in Malaysia must comply with a comprehensive legal and regulatory framework governing employment relationships. Key obligations cover employment contracts, wages, working hours, leave entitlements, termination procedures, payroll administration, and mandatory statutory contributions.

Tax, Accounting, and Audit

 Published: September 2026

Once established in Malaysia, businesses must comply with the country’s tax, accounting, and financial reporting framework. Depending on their activities and structure, this may include corporate income tax, Sales and Service Tax (SST), withholding tax, e-invoicing, statutory reporting, and audit obligations.
