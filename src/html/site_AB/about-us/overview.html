About Us

Asia Briefing provides practical, up-to-date business intelligence on the legal, tax, regulatory, and operational issues affecting international companies across Asia. Founded in 1999, Asia Briefing is Ascentium's business intelligence platform covering key investment markets across the region.

Our Business Publications

Asia Briefing's portfolio includes China Briefing, India Briefing, Vietnam Briefing, and ASEAN Briefing Briefing, providing market-specific coverage alongside broader regional analysis. Our publications include articles, magazines, guides, and reports for international businesses operating across Asia.

Asia Briefing draws on the expertise of Dezan Shira & Associates, an Ascentium company, whose professionals work directly with international businesses across the region. This gives our publications access to practical insight from consultants, lawyers, accountants, human resource professionals, and other specialists working across the markets we cover.
