About Us

India Briefing provides practical, up-to-date insight into the legal, tax, and operational issues affecting foreign investors and companies operating in or entering India. First published in 2007, the publication is part of Asia Briefing, Ascentium's business intelligence platform covering key investment markets across Asia.

Discover Our Business Publications

Alongside India Briefing, Asia Briefing's portfolio includes ASEAN, India, Indonesia, and Vietnam Briefing, with magazines, guides, and reports available through our publications store.

Asia Briefing draws on the expertise of Dezan Shira & Associates, an Ascentium company, whose professionals work directly with international businesses across Asia. This connection gives our publications access to practical insight from consultants, lawyers, accountants, human resource professionals, and other specialists working across the markets we cover.
